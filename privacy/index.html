Alexandrio Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

About Alexandrio

Alexandrio is a personal reading library that allows users to organize books, notes, definitions, and other reading-related information.

Information We Collect

Alexandrio may collect account information such as your email address and user identifier. The app also stores content you choose to create, including your library information, notes, definitions, ratings, and reading information.

How We Use Information

Your information is used to provide and operate Alexandrio, maintain your account, store your reading information, and provide the app's features.

Service Providers

Alexandrio uses third-party services to provide features such as authentication, data storage, book information, and dictionary information.

Advertising and Sale of Data

Alexandrio does not sell your personal information and does not use your information for third-party advertising or tracking.

Data Retention and Deletion

Your information is retained while your Alexandrio account is active or as needed to provide the service. You may delete your Alexandrio account from within the app. Account deletion removes the personal data associated with your account from Alexandrio's systems.

Data Export

Alexandrio provides an account data export feature within the app.

Security

Alexandrio uses reasonable technical measures and third-party infrastructure to protect user information. No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Alexandrio changes. Updates will be reflected by the date shown at the top of this page.
